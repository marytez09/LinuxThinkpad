# Full Disassembly and Thermal Paste Re-application 
## SAFETY!! Remove power cable and battery before manipulating components

## Full Disassembly Process
(All screws were removed with a Phillip Screwdriver)
- Flipped over computer and removed back screws 

[Removed Screws](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2235.jpg)

- Flipped computer back around, slide keyboard upwards and remove keyboard ribbon
    - Removed trackpad ribbon (flipped white switch upwards)

[Keyboard Ribbon](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2236.jpg)

[Keyboard Ribbon Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2237.jpg)

- Removed the Base Panel (trackpad) and disconnected ribbon.

[Base Panel Ribbon](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2238.jpg)

[Base Panel Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2239.jpg)

- Unplugged display ribbon

[Display Ribbon](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2240.jpg) 

- Unplugged antenna cables black wire (AUX) & gray wire (MAIN)

[Antenna Cables](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2241.jpg)

- Unscrewed WLAN card and remove

[WLAN card](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2245.jpg)

- Unscrewed plastic plate (2 screws) and remove plastic covering

[Plastic Plate Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2246.jpg)

- Unscrewed blue-tooth module

[Blue-Tooth Module](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2247.jpg)

[Blue-Tooth Module Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2248.jpg)

- Unplugged speaker cable

[Speaker Cable Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2250.jpg)

- Removed 4 screws securing motherboard and remove 
    - Unlatched 2nd display ribbon

[2nd Display Ribbon](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2252.jpg)

- Removed power connector (underneath motherboard)

[Power Connector](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2253.jpg)

[Motherboard Removed](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2254.jpg)

[Motherboard Component](/Images/Cleaning-and-Thermal-Paste/IMG_2255.jpg)

- Unscrewed 4 screws securing CPU cooler and remove
    - Flipped upside down, removed 1 screw securing GPU cooler
    - Removed fan connector

## Thermal Paste Re-Application
- Dipped a small Q-tip in isopropyl alcohol

- Removed old thermal paste off of CPU and heat sink

- Applied new thermal paste to CPU

## Reassembly
- Re-installed everything back together
    - Reconnected internal cables
    - Re-installed internal components
    - Re-installed bottom cover
    - Re-installed battery
